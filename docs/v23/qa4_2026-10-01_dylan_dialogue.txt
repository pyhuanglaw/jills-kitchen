2026-10-01 — Dylan × Jill dialogue revision for v2.3 (from the player/designer, filed verbatim in substance)

1. DELETE the cooler scene completely:
   Dylan「冷藏庫。」 Jill「嗯。」 Dylan「好，那我不問了。」
   No replacement cooler scene in this release. The exchange has no understandable conversational premise.

2. Post-reveal 王太太 exchange:
   王太太「追到了沒？」 Dylan「還在努力。」 Jill「不要理他。」   (was: Jill「十一年了。」)

3. Writing rule for Jill × Dylan (going forward):
   Convey the relationship through shorthand, shared habits, unfinished sentences the player still understands,
   knowing each other's routines, teasing, ordinary domestic familiarity, Dylan still "pursuing" his own wife,
   Jill acting unimpressed while clearly in on the joke.
   Avoid dialogue whose main purpose is to tell the player "they have been together eleven years" or
   "they are a loving long-term couple" — let the player infer it. The story title / character information
   can carry that fact.
   Dylan can be considerate but is not a flat, constantly gentle perfect romantic lead.
   Jill can call him annoying or brush him off, but never reads as genuinely cold toward him.
   Test: if the same exchange could be given to almost any generic romantic NPC, it is not specific enough.
   Good examples already in the game: 「老樣子？」「嗯。」 / 「老闆娘，晚上一起吃飯？」「回家吃。」「好。」 /
   「老闆娘，明天有空嗎？」「不行，我老公會生氣。」「那確實滿麻煩的。」 / the reveal 「老公，走了。」「好。」

4. Revised lines:
   「老闆娘，我可以追妳嗎？」「你不是在追？」「那我繼續。」            (was: 「追到了再說。」「那就是可以。」)
   「老闆娘，妳一個人住嗎？」「跟五隻貓，還有一個很煩的人。」「誰？」「你。」   (was: 「聽起來滿熱鬧的。」)

5. Anniversary:
   「紀念日想吃什麼？」「你決定。」「我每次決定妳都說不要。」「所以你先想三個。」
   (was: 「老闆娘，紀念日想吃什麼？」「你記得日期？」「我只是問問。」「……那天店裡吃。」 — it made Dylan sound like
   a husband who may not remember the date)

6. Do NOT broadly rewrite the rest of the Dylan/Jill dialogue in this task. Keep the lines that already work.
