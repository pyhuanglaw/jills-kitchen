Jill’s Kitchen v2.3 — Normal-Play QA + Story Visibility + Late-Game Depth Pass

Use the current v2.3 RC1 as the baseline.

This is a focused follow-up based on real iPhone normal play around Day 48.

IMPORTANT:
- Do NOT rewrite the game.
- Do NOT replace the existing v2.3 Story Facts / Relationship Facts / Story Event Arbiter architecture.
- Do NOT create a second narrative simulation.
- Preserve mature saves and all existing history.
- Preserve the single-file playable Artifact output.
- This pass should make existing systems more visible, usable, spatially rich, and rewarding in normal play.
- Fix real observed UX/gameplay issues before adding unnecessary infrastructure.
- Do not spend excessive time on pixel-perfect automated screenshot tooling. Real mobile playability matters more.

==================================================
A. CORE PRODUCT OBSERVATION
==================================================

v2.3 has substantially more story and simulation underneath, but normal play still hides too much of it.

The player should be able to KNOW:

“This character story is progressing.”

without being told:

“Perform action X twice to unlock event Y.”

New design principle:

SHOW HOW FAR THE STORY HAS GONE.
DO NOT SHOW HOW TO FORCE THE NEXT BEAT.

The player may miss a live presentation during a busy service.
The player must NOT permanently miss the story because of that.

“You may miss the performance, but you must not miss the story.”

==================================================
B. STORY MAP / STORY PROGRESS
==================================================

Add a clearly visible “故事” section to the Restaurant Journal.

This is NOT a quest checklist and NOT an RPG mission system.

No:
- “Talk to Mia 2/3 times”
- “Seat Sophie beside Mia”
- claim-reward buttons
- XP rewards
- affection meters
- exclamation marks over characters
- visible trigger conditions
- hidden-system terminology such as madeSpace / choseNear
- fake progress generated from pre-v2.3 history

Instead, show authored STORY LINES and their discovered beats.

Two categories:

1. 餐廳故事 / Restaurant Story
2. 人物故事 / Character Stories

--------------------------------------------------
B1. Character Story presentation
--------------------------------------------------

Example:

Sophie & Mia
「慢慢變得不一樣」

●━━●━━●━━○━━○━━○━━○━━○

3 / 8 個故事片段

DAY XX
「開始記得彼此」

DAY XX
「今天坐得近了一點」

DAY XX
「留了一個位置」

Next:
？？？

「她們的故事還在繼續。」

The player should clearly know:
- this is a real continuing story line;
- it has progressed;
- more remains.

But the player should NOT know the mechanical requirements for the next beat.

Use existing real Story Facts/events as the source of truth.
Do not invent completed beats.

Important:
The progress count should correspond to meaningful authored story milestones, NOT every ambient callback.

Ambient/repeat dialogue should not inflate story progress.

--------------------------------------------------
B2. Dylan
--------------------------------------------------

Before the reveal, the line may appear as something like:

Jill & Dylan
「那位常來的客人」

The player should see that something is developing, without being told the truth.

Possible discovered-beat language should reflect only what the player has actually observed:
- 又來了
- Jill 記得他的習慣
- 五隻貓好像很熟悉他
etc.

When the existing husband reveal genuinely occurs, update the story title/presentation.

After reveal:

Jill & Dylan
「結婚十一年，還在追」

CRITICAL:
The reveal is NOT “story complete”.

Dylan’s story continues after the reveal:
Valentine’s Day, waiting after closing, regular-life callbacks, shared restaurant history, etc.

Being revealed as Jill’s husband must not make Dylan less romantic.

--------------------------------------------------
B3. Ken × Monsieur 杜
--------------------------------------------------

Make the nature of this line clear:

Ken & Monsieur 杜
「總是談不攏的兩個人」
友情故事

Show meaningful discovered milestones.

Never imply romance.
Do not use generic intimacy = romance logic.

Their required friendship Story Photo can appear as one of the meaningful milestones when genuinely unlocked.

--------------------------------------------------
B4. Other lines
--------------------------------------------------

Use the existing authored v2.3 arcs where appropriate:
- 晴 × 阿拓
- Sophie × 寶寶
- 王先生 × 王太太
- Jill × Dylan
- Ken × Monsieur 杜
- Sophie × Mia
- other genuinely authored persistent arcs already in the game

Do not manufacture story lines merely to fill the page.

Some undiscovered lines may appear as silhouettes / “？？？” if useful, so the player understands that more stories exist.

But avoid excessive spoilers.

==================================================
D. STORY UPDATE NOTIFICATION
==================================================

When a meaningful authored story milestone occurs, give the player a subtle, non-blocking notification:

故事更新
Sophie & Mia
「留了一個位置」
3 / 8

or equivalent.

The notification should NOT pause service.

Tapping it should deep-link to that story in the Restaurant Journal.

This is especially important during busy service.

If the player misses the dialogue bubble/event visually, the Story Journal still preserves the meaningful development.

Do not notify for every ambient callback.
Only meaningful story progression.

==================================================
E. DIALOGUE HISTORY — CURRENT SYSTEM LOSES IMPORTANT CONTENT
==================================================

Observed on iPhone:

During busy service, dialogue can accumulate very quickly — sometimes 30–40 lines.

The current visible dialogue history appears too short (roughly the latest ~14), so older dialogue becomes inaccessible.

Some NPC dialogue also appears not to reliably enter the history.

This is now unacceptable because v2.3 dialogue can carry real story progression, especially:
- Dylan
- Sophie / Mia
- Ken / Monsieur 杜
- Lounge staff
- other authored arcs

Fix this structurally.

Requirements:

1. Keep the COMPLETE dialogue history for the current service/day, not only ~14 recent lines.

2. Make it scrollable and mobile-friendly.

3. Ensure meaningful named-NPC/story dialogue reliably enters the history.

4. Important story-progress dialogue should additionally be preserved through the Story Journal milestone/history where appropriate.

5. Ordinary repeated ambient chatter does not need permanent multi-day storage.

6. Avoid duplicate spam.

7. Do not let the dialogue log become the source of story truth.
Story Facts remain source of truth.

==================================================
F. DIALOGUE PANEL MOBILE CLOSE CONTROL
==================================================

Observed:
Closing the dialogue panel currently requires tapping the small dialogue bubble again.

On iPhone, especially during rush, this is frustrating and difficult.

Add a clear visible × close button inside the dialogue panel.

Requirements:
- comfortably tappable mobile hit target;
- closes immediately;
- does not require repeatedly finding/tapping the original speech-bubble button;
- does not interfere with scrolling dialogue history.

==================================================
G. RESTAURANT JOURNAL DISCOVERABILITY
==================================================

The Restaurant Journal is now one of the most important systems in the game:
reviews, regulars, dialogue/history, Album, cats, achievements, restaurant history, and now Story.

It is currently too difficult to locate consistently across game phases.

Fix the information architecture.

At minimum:

- Add “餐廳日誌” directly to the Pause menu.
- It should be accessible consistently during service.
- It should remain accessible during prep / after closing / evening management wherever technically reasonable.
- Do not make the player remember which phase exposes it.
- Preserve existing Journal content.
- Add the new Story section cleanly rather than creating another disconnected hidden menu.

The Journal should feel like the restaurant’s permanent record.

==================================================
H. SOCIAL / MARKETING DISCOVERABILITY
==================================================

Current implementation exists, but real play showed a serious discoverability problem.

The player knew that Social / Marketing had been implemented and STILL could not find it.

Current path is effectively:
close restaurant → evening upgrade/shop → horizontally scroll deep into shop tabs → 社群與宣傳.

That is too hidden for a recurring management system.

Audit its placement.

Do NOT delete the existing system.

Find a clearer access point / navigation treatment so the player can reasonably discover and revisit:
- social posts;
- what people are talking about;
- Jill post candidates;
- active campaign;
- campaign results.

It may still be editable/launchable at an appropriate management time, but it should not feel like a secret submenu inside furniture/upgrades.

Do not duplicate the state/system.

==================================================
I. KITCHEN / LATE-GAME EQUIPMENT DEPTH
==================================================

Observed around Day 48:
Kitchen equipment feels too sparse.
The player has substantial money and too few meaningful things to buy.

Audit late-game kitchen progression.

Add meaningful equipment/upgrade depth where it genuinely improves gameplay/space.

In particular:
- refrigerator/cold storage should have further meaningful capacity upgrade(s);
- consider additional high-end kitchen equipment / workflow improvements;
- equipment should be visually represented where appropriate;
- avoid adding meaningless +5% stat clutter;
- avoid turning the game into inventory micromanagement.

The restaurant should LOOK and FEEL more developed after major investment.

The player currently reports:
“I have nowhere to spend money.”

Treat this as an economy/progression problem, not merely “add one expensive decoration.”

==================================================
J. SIDE HALL — WINDOW CAT LIFE
==================================================

Observed:
The Side Hall has a large window area with almost nothing happening on it.

This is wasted spatial storytelling.

Add purchasable indoor cat window furniture.

Examples:
- window-mounted cat shelf;
- padded window perch;
- multi-level window walk;
- suspended/window hammock;
- premium multi-cat observation platform.

These are INDOOR.

Cats may autonomously:
- jump up;
- sleep there;
- sit and look outside;
- move between levels;
- occasionally share nearby levels/perches.

Do NOT add:
- cat chores;
- cat hunger/happiness meters;
- required cat management.

This should be:
player improves restaurant → cats gain richer autonomous life → player gets to notice it.

It should also provide meaningful late-game money sinks.

==================================================
K. MAIN HALL LIGHTING
==================================================

Observed on real iPhone:
After purchasing the expensive Main Hall lighting upgrade, the upper half appears brighter but the lower half can look comparatively DARKER.

This makes an expensive visual upgrade feel worse.

Audit actual rendered lighting on mobile.

Fix illumination/falloff/overlay so:
- the expensive upgrade visibly improves the room;
- the lower half is not unintentionally darkened;
- the room retains warmth/depth and is not flattened into uniform brightness.

Do not solve this merely by globally increasing canvas brightness.

==================================================
L. MOBILE TICKET / ROOM TAB OVERLAP
==================================================

Observed:
The top row of customer/order needs can sometimes be obscured by the room-switching controls.

This is a real mobile UI collision.

Fix responsive layout/zoning so room tabs never cover actionable customer/ticket information.

Test at iPhone-like viewport around 390×844.

==================================================
M. SIDE HALL TABLE / SEATING CONSISTENCY
==================================================

Observed:
- the first/top row Side Hall tables still appear noticeably larger;
- Main Hall seating can be upgraded to support more people, while Side Hall does not behave consistently.

Audit Side Hall table sizing and seating progression.

If Side Hall intentionally uses larger group-dining tables, make that design rule clear and coherent.

Otherwise, align furniture progression with Main Hall conventions.

Do not leave accidental visual/functional inconsistency.

==================================================
N. LIFE ALBUM — OVERLAPPING CAT PHOTO BUG
==================================================

Observed:
The photo caption says 小齁 and 寶寶 are squeezed together on the same cushion, but visually only 寶寶 is visible because the sprites overlap.

Fix this.

Album rule remains:

If the caption says two specific cats are visibly sharing a moment, both cats must actually be visually readable in the captured photo.

For this composition:
- offset bodies/heads enough to read both identities;
- preserve the “squeezed together” feeling;
- do not simply separate them so far that the moment disappears.

Add regression coverage if practical.

==================================================
O. DOG GUEST / DOG HOUSE VISUAL PASS
==================================================

Observed on iPhone:
- the dog house/waiting area looks crude;
- it sits too far left and can be partially clipped by the viewport;
- every visiting dog appears effectively the same;
- the current dog silhouette can read more like a mouse than a dog.

Redesign this feature.

Create at least FIVE visibly distinct dog archetypes.

Do NOT make five recolors of one sprite.

Use clearly different silhouettes/body proportions, for example:

1. Small fluffy dog
   - Pomeranian-like
   - compact round body
   - fluffy curled tail
   - small pointed ears

2. Small long-bodied dog
   - dachshund-like
   - short legs
   - long torso
   - long/floppy ears

3. Medium mixed-breed dog
   - natural proportions
   - semi-pointed ears
   - curved tail
   - friendly everyday neighborhood-dog feel

4. Medium floppy-ear dog
   - softer face
   - hanging ears
   - slightly stockier body

5. Large retriever-like dog
   - clearly larger scale
   - longer legs/body
   - broad friendly head
   - feathered/relaxed tail

They do not need to represent literal copyrighted/brand-specific breeds.
They need readable DOG silhouettes and variety.

Optional low-cost idle behavior:
- sit;
- lie down;
- tail wag;
- look toward restaurant/owner.

Do NOT create a dog-management subsystem.

Redesign the dog waiting spot / dog house so it looks intentional and appropriate to Jill’s Kitchen.

Ensure safe responsive margins:
nothing important should be clipped on iPhone.
