PHASE 7 — CONTENT DIRECTION RECAP

Continue the current v2.3 execution. Do NOT stop for approval.

Before implementing Phase 7, use the following as the intended character/story direction. These are not requests for a new narrative framework. Use the Story Facts / Relationship Facts / Story Event Arbiter already implemented in Phases 1–6.

The purpose of Phase 7 is not “more dialogue.”

The purpose is:

The restaurant has been open long enough that people begin to know the cats, Jill, the staff, and each other.

The player should gradually feel:

「表面上經營 Jill 的餐廳，
實際上是在偷看 Jill、五隻貓和一群熟客慢慢過日子。」

CORE STORY RULE

A good story unit is:

real history
→ recognition / callback
→ visible behavior or event
→ something is slightly different afterward

Whenever possible:

「因為這件事發生過，所以以後有一點不一樣。」

Do not turn the game into an RPG:
- no quest list
- no relationship meters
- no “Sophie story 3/8”
- no exclamation marks
- no claim rewards
- no visible friendship XP

Facts and familiarity remain invisible.

Most days should still feel like normal restaurant life.

Target rhythm:
- usually 0–1 major authored beat per day
- 0–2 minor callbacks
- ambient familiarity can happen naturally
- some days should have no story beat at all

Normal restaurant life remains ~80%.
Memorable authored life is ~20%.

==================================================
1. SOPHIE × MIA — MAIN SLOW ROMANCE
==================================================

This is the primary developing romance.

IMPORTANT:
The first half should be almost indistinguishable from friendship.

Do NOT make repeated co-presence alone equal romance.

Meaningful progression comes from ACTIVE CHOICES.

Intended progression:

A. They repeatedly recognize each other.
「妳也常來？」

B. They become comfortable sharing space.

C. Sophie chooses a seat near Mia even when other seats exist.

D. Mia arrives and Sophie quietly moves her bag off the adjacent chair.

E. They share food.

F. Sophie has already finished eating but stays because Mia has not arrived yet.

Evan may notice this and say nothing.

G. Eventually they leave together.

This may create an understated Album moment:
《今天一起走》

H. Much later, they arrive together.

Jill:
「今天一起？」

Mia:
「嗯。」

Sophie:
「……怎樣？」

Jill:
「沒有啊。」

Do NOT create a dramatic confession requirement.

The relationship becomes clear because their behavior changes repeatedly.

They always remain two independent customers:
- separate identities
- separate visit histories
- separate preferences
- separate story histories

Never merge them into a generic “couple” guest.

Sophie × 寶寶 continues independently.
Mia only knows about Sophie × 寶寶 history if she legitimately witnessed or learned it.

Romance must only emerge from authored romantic-eligible facts.
The generic relationship system must NEVER generate romance.

A genuine later milestone qualifies for a canonical Story Photo, but only after that history actually happened.

2. KEN × MONSIEUR 杜 — DEEP FRIENDSHIP, NOT ROMANCE
==================================================

This relationship is intentionally a contrast to Sophie × Mia.

Ken and Monsieur 杜 can become extremely familiar:

- argue about wine
- know each other's preferences
- sit together
- save an adjacent seat
- notice when the other is absent
- wait for each other
- order together
- arrive separately but spend the evening together

Example tone:

晴:
「你今天不等杜先生？」

Ken:
「我為什麼要等他？」

pause

Ken:
「他有說幾點嗎？」

This must NEVER later become a romance reveal.

The point is:

親密 ≠ 戀愛。

Ken × 杜 require a FRIENDSHIP Story Photo after genuine shared history.

Possible understated concept/title:
《還是沒有同意》

They are at their familiar Lounge spot, different wine glasses, shared snack, still disagreeing about something.

The image and writing must never imply romance.

If the relationship genuinely develops, it can eventually earn a relationship Story Photo.

==================================================

He genuinely loves her.

The only thing he “pretends” is that he still has not successfully won her over.

Before the reveal, his behavior should make the player increasingly think:

「你很閒是不是？」

==================================================

Story meaning is fixed.

- preferred seat unavailable
- exact room unavailable
- Jill busy
- one optional staff member absent
- a dish is not currently on the menu

But never fake required CHARACTER presence.

If a character-specific beat genuinely requires that character, delay the beat.

Critical long-term arcs may use the already implemented overdue/catch-up weighting.

Do NOT solve this with hardcoded Day X story schedules.
