==================================================
IMPORTANT PAYOFF — MONSIEUR DU × COLLABORATION WINE
==================================================

There is one important payoff I want to preserve:

Ken and Monsieur Du do NOT simply agree with each other about wine.

Part of their existing relationship is that they have different opinions,
different preferences, and may disagree with each other's judgments.

KEEP THAT.

Do not flatten their relationship into:
"two wine experts who always agree."

Their disagreement is part of why their interactions are interesting.

However, AFTER the Jill's Kitchen × Ken collaboration wine has been completed
and permanently added to the wine list, I want a later Monsieur Du beat:

MONSIEUR DU DRINKS THE COLLABORATION WINE.

This should pay off their earlier disagreements.

==================================================
THE IMPORTANT MOMENT
==================================================

Monsieur Du should taste the collaboration wine seriously.

Ken knows Monsieur Du well enough by this point to expect criticism.

Do not make the scene melodramatic.

Do not have Ken anxiously ask:
「怎麼樣？好喝嗎？」

Do not make Monsieur Du suddenly overly enthusiastic.

Let the tension come from their established relationship.

Monsieur Du tastes it.

He may initially make one small technical / stylistic observation,
especially if that fits their previous disagreements.

Then, after a beat, he gives it a clear and genuine compliment.

The important thing is:

MONSIEUR DU ACTUALLY THINKS THE WINE IS GOOD.

Do not undermine the payoff with:
「還可以。」
「勉強及格。」
「至少能喝。」
or a joke that effectively cancels the compliment.

His praise can still be restrained because that fits him,
but it must be unmistakably sincere.

For example, tone only:

杜先生喝了一口，沒有立刻說話。

Ken：「怎麼？」

杜先生：「我還是不會照你這個方向選。」

Ken：「我就知道。」

杜先生又喝了一口。

杜先生：「但這支很好。」

Ken looks at him.

杜先生：「真的很好。」

Do NOT copy this mechanically.
Use their established voices and existing relationship history.

The emotional payoff is:

They STILL disagree about wine.

Monsieur Du has NOT suddenly accepted Ken's entire philosophy.

But he respects what Ken and Jill made.

That distinction matters.

==================================================
WHY THIS MATTERS
==================================================

The collaboration wine should become a physical record of Ken's story:

Ken helped inspire the Lounge
→ Ken began hosting tastings
→ Ken and Jill learned what works in this restaurant
→ after three tastings they created their own collaboration wine
→ Monsieur Du, who often disagrees with Ken, eventually tastes it
→ and sincerely says it is good.

This gives the collaboration wine meaning beyond stats.

The player remembers:

"This is the wine even Monsieur Du praised."

That is much more valuable than simply giving it a large gameplay bonus.

==================================================
DO NOT MAKE THIS IMMEDIATE
==================================================

Monsieur Du should NOT conveniently appear the same night the collaboration
wine is released just to deliver the payoff.

Let the wine exist in the restaurant first.

Then, on a later valid visit / story beat,
Monsieur Du encounters it naturally.

This should feel like a callback, not an achievement ceremony.

==================================================
AFTERWARD — AMBIENT CALLBACK
==================================================

After this authored beat has happened, allow VERY LOW-FREQUENCY callbacks.

For example, on a future visit Monsieur Du may order the collaboration wine
without making a big deal about it.

That is enough.

The strongest callback may simply be:

he orders it again.

Do not repeatedly explain:
"this is the wine Monsieur Du once praised."

Trust the player's memory.

==================================================
STORY STATE
==================================================

This beat requires:

- collaboration wine completed
- collaboration wine available
- sufficient Ken × Monsieur Du relationship/story history for the disagreement
  and respect to make sense

It must NOT occur before their relationship has established that they sometimes
disagree about wine.

If the current Ken × Du 8-beat arc already contains specific disagreements,
reuse that history rather than inventing duplicate conflicts.

Please inspect the existing implementation first and place this payoff at the
most narratively appropriate point.

If necessary, this can become a post-8/8 epilogue/callback rather than changing
the existing 8-beat structure.

Do not force it into 8/8 if doing so damages the current arc.

==================================================
KEY RULE
==================================================

Do not resolve Ken × Monsieur Du by making them agree.

Resolve this particular moment by showing that:

THEY CAN DISAGREE WITH EACH OTHER
AND STILL RECOGNIZE WHEN THE OTHER PERSON HAS MADE SOMETHING GOOD.
