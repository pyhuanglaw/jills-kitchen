SECOND FLOOR VISUAL / SPATIAL REFERENCE UPDATE

I now have the visual reference for the Second Floor.

Please treat the supplied image as the canonical reference for:

SECOND FLOOR — PHASE 0 / PHASE 1

It defines:
- the overall second-floor architecture
- staircase position
- windows
- columns
- floor proportions
- initial vacant state
- Missing Cats scene
- newly acquired open-floor state
- early movable furniture
- early human/cat use

IMPORTANT:

The image intentionally does NOT show the later Staff Room or Private Dining Room partitions.

Do NOT interpret this to mean the second floor remains permanently open-plan.

The correct architectural progression is:

VACANT OPEN FLOOR
→
JILL ACQUIRES WHOLE SECOND FLOOR
→
EARLY OPEN-FLOOR USE
→
STAFF ROOM STORY
→
REAL STAFF ROOM CONSTRUCTION
→
PRIVATE DINING STORY
→
REAL PRIVATE DINING ROOM CONSTRUCTION

The architecture must physically evolve over time.


==================================================
1. USE THIS IMAGE AS THE BASE FLOOR
==================================================

Treat the supplied Second Floor image as the canonical base geometry.

Preserve as closely as technically practical:

- staircase / stair opening
- railing
- windows
- structural columns
- overall floor proportions
- circulation logic
- main open-floor character

Do not redesign the second floor into a different building.

The first acquired state should look very close to this reference.


==================================================
2. WHY THERE ARE NO PARTITIONS YET
==================================================

This is intentional.

When Jill first leases the second floor, she is leasing:

THE FLOOR.

She is not simultaneously building every future room.

At first, Jill only performs basic work:

- cleaning
- safety
- electrical
- lighting
- floor/wall maintenance
- climate control
- staircase safety

Then the whole floor can exist as an open usable area for some time.

The player should have a period where they experience:

「二樓已經是 Jill’s Kitchen 的了，
但還沒有被決定成什麼。」

This is an important part of the progression.


==================================================
3. EARLY FURNITURE IS TEMPORARY / MOVABLE
==================================================

The reference shows early furniture such as:

- shared wooden table
- mismatched chairs
- utility cabinet
- coat/bag stand
- lamp
- plant
- small cat cushion/perch/scratching object

Treat these as movable furniture.

They do NOT define permanent room boundaries.

When later construction occurs, they may:

- move
- be reused
- migrate into Staff Room where appropriate
- disappear if replaced by better furniture

Do not build future architecture around their exact temporary positions.


==================================================
4. MISSING CATS EVENT
==================================================

Use the reference as visual direction for the Missing Cats authored beat.

Canonical event:

Near closing, Jill / present employees notice 柔柔 is missing.

Then they realize 小齁 is also missing.

They immediately search.

They discover the upstairs access is not fully latched.

This occurred that day / recently.

DO NOT imply the door was open for several days.

The cats have only been unaccounted for for a believable short period.

Jill and the ACTUALLY PRESENT closing staff go upstairs.

They find:

柔柔
+
小齁

safe inside the vacant second floor.

柔柔 should plausibly be comfortable near the window / exploring.

小齁 is nearby.

They have NOT escaped outside.

The scene begins with real concern and ends with relief + mild comedy.

This is also the player's first memorable experience of the scale of the second floor.

Do not immediately unlock the expansion after this event.

Existing canonical game assets win.


==================================================
6. EARLY OPEN-FLOOR LIFE
==================================================

After Jill eventually acquires the whole floor:

Do NOT immediately trigger Staff Room.

Allow the second floor to exist in its open state for a meaningful period.

During this period:

- Jill may occasionally use it
- staff may briefly sit / place something there
- cats may safely visit
- the shared table may get used casually
- movable objects gradually appear

But:

THIS IS NOT THE STAFF ROOM.

Do not silently give the open floor all Staff Room functionality.


==================================================
7. IMPORTANT PRODUCT BEHAVIOR
==================================================

The open second floor should actually help make the later Staff Room story stronger.

Example:

A staff member briefly sits at the shared table.

Someone leaves a bag there.

Someone charges a phone there.

An outside friend/relative waits there once access is appropriate.

This creates the feeling:

people are already trying to use the new space.

But the space still lacks:

- privacy
- proper employee storage
- comfortable permanent seating
- dedicated break facilities
- employee fridge
- lockers
- appropriate rest environment

So the player can feel:

「二樓有了，但這還不等於員工有休息室。」

That distinction matters.


==================================================
8. STAFF ROOM MUST BECOME A REAL ROOM
==================================================

When the Staff Room Restaurant Story eventually reaches:

《大家待的地方》

and the player purchases the Staff Room project:

ACTUAL CONSTRUCTION must occur.

Do NOT implement Staff Room as:

- sofa placed in corner
- Staff Room furniture spawned in open floor
- invisible zone
- menu-only room
- floor mat defining a room
- open-plan employee corner

It must become an architectural room.


==================================================
9. STAFF ROOM CONSTRUCTION
==================================================

Use one appropriate side / corner of the canonical second-floor geometry.

Add:

- real partition walls
- real doorway
- closable door
- appropriate interior lighting
- enough acoustic / visual separation to feel like an employee room

Preserve:

- central circulation
- stair access
- windows where appropriate
- remaining usable second-floor area
- future Private Dining Room possibility

The Staff Room must NOT consume the entire floor.

The player should visibly see:

BEFORE:
open floor

AFTER:
one part of the floor has now become a room.


==================================================
10. STAFF ROOM SHOULD HAVE A DOOR
==================================================

This is important.

Employees need a place that is genuinely separate from customer/public restaurant space.

The Staff Room should feel:

「進去休息。」

not:

「坐在二樓角落。」

A door also creates useful story staging:

- 宇翔 can wait inside
- 珊珊 can wait inside
- 國雄 can be invited inside
- Jill can enter
- staff can come and go
- cats may enter only when appropriate

The doorway itself becomes part of restaurant life.
