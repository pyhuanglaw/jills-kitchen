IMPORTANT CORRECTION — AUTHORED STORY PRESENTATION

Please revise my previous narrative-timing instruction.

I do NOT want the solution to be:
“important stories should generally be moved outside restaurant service.”

That was the wrong framing.

The actual problem is:

WHEN AN IMPORTANT AUTHORED STORY BEAT OCCURS,
THE PLAYER SHOULD NOT HAVE TO MANAGE THE RESTAURANT AT THE SAME TIME.

==================================================
NEW CORE RULE
==================================================

Important authored story beats MAY happen during active restaurant service.

In fact, some of them SHOULD happen during service when the restaurant/work context is narratively important.

However:

WHEN A MEANINGFUL AUTHORED STORY BEAT STARTS:

→ PAUSE / FREEZE ACTIVE RESTAURANT GAMEPLAY
→ stop gameplay time from progressing
→ stop order timers / service pressure from progressing
→ present the story clearly
→ require PLAYER INPUT to advance dialogue / pages
→ do NOT auto-advance meaningful dialogue
→ after the authored beat is finished, resume the restaurant exactly where it paused

The player should be able to stop managing the restaurant, read the story comfortably, manually advance it, and then return to service.

The player should NEVER need to think:

“Was there just a story happening while I was serving customers?”

or:

“I need to keep watching the screen because a story might suddenly disappear.”

==================================================
PLAYER-CONTROLLED ADVANCEMENT
==================================================

For meaningful authored dialogue:

DO NOT let important lines disappear automatically.

The player must explicitly:
- tap / click to continue
- advance to the next line / page / dialogue state

The story remains on screen until the player advances it.

If there is a Story Illustration / Event CG:
- pause gameplay
- keep it visible
- player explicitly continues/closes it

The player controls reading speed.

No important authored story should be lost because the player was:
- serving food
- reading orders
- switching rooms
- managing inventory
- handling Lounge
- looking somewhere else on screen

==================================================
DURING-SERVICE STORIES
==================================================

Do NOT move a story outside service merely because it is important.

Inspect the actual content.

If the restaurant operating is part of the scene, KEEP IT during service.

Example:

沈晴 × 阿拓

If their relationship develops through:
- bartending together
- covering each other's work
- passing things
- noticing each other's workflow
- helping during service
- work-related chemistry

those beats SHOULD remain during Lounge service.

When a meaningful authored beat triggers:
pause gameplay → show the scene → player advances → resume service.

This preserves BOTH:
- the correct narrative context
- the player's ability to actually read it

==================================================
CUSTOMER STORIES
==================================================

Same principle.

If a customer story naturally happens:
- when arriving
- when seated
- while ordering
- while eating
- while interacting with another customer
- because of something happening in the restaurant

KEEP IT there.

Pause gameplay for the meaningful authored portion.

Do not artificially keep everyone until closing just to make story presentation easier.

==================================================
AFTER-CLOSING / BEFORE-OPENING STORIES
==================================================

These windows are STILL useful, but only when the CONTENT naturally belongs there.

For example:

《那面牆》

Some substantial discussions may naturally happen after closing:
- looking carefully at photos/evidence
- Mia discussing repair traces
- Sophie reconstructing the timeline
- 王先生 discussing suspicion vs proof
- legal next steps
- 怡君 making decisions

If after closing is narratively natural, use it.

Likewise Second Floor exploration/searching for 柔柔 and 小齁 may naturally fit near/after closing.

But:

DO NOT move scenes after closing simply because it is technically easier.

Story context determines WHEN the event happens.

The pause/manual-advance system ensures the player can read it even if the correct context is during service.

==================================================
CRITICAL DISTINCTION: AUTHORED STORY vs AMBIENT LIFE
==================================================

AUTHORED STORY BEAT:
- meaningful progression
- important character development
- relationship progression
- story facts the player should understand
- important dialogue
- Story Illustration / Event CG

→ PAUSE GAMEPLAY
→ PLAYER-CONTROLLED ADVANCEMENT
→ NOT MISSABLE

AMBIENT LIFE:
- cat interactions
- Jill ↔ cat micro-interactions
- customer ↔ cat behavior
- tiny staff interactions
- casual greetings
- one-line flavor dialogue
- visual workplace chemistry
- ordinary restaurant life
- callbacks that do not advance important story state

→ DO NOT pause gameplay
→ may occur in realtime
→ may be missable
→ should make the restaurant feel alive

Do not pause the restaurant every time somebody says one casual sentence.

==================================================
IMPORTANT UX REQUIREMENT
==================================================

The transition into an authored story must be unmistakable.

When it begins, the player should immediately understand:

“The restaurant is paused. I can read this now.”

Do not leave active order timers visually appearing to run behind the story.

Do not require the player to rush through dialogue because customers are waiting.

Do not automatically resume until the authored sequence is actually complete.

After completion:
restore the exact gameplay state safely and resume normal service.
