KEN / LOUNGE STORY CONTINUITY PASS
Priority: please do this before expanding unrelated P5 staff stories.

I found a narrative continuity problem in the current game:

Ken is a sommelier / wine professional and is an important character in the Lounge becoming part of Jill's Kitchen.

But after the Lounge is actually built, Ken currently does not have enough story that follows from that change.

This feels wrong.

Ken should not function as:
Ken appears → helps trigger Lounge → Lounge is built → Ken's role is finished → later Ken × Monsieur Du suddenly happens.

The Lounge being completed should instead be the BEGINNING of the next stage of Ken's story.

==================================================
CORE STORY CHAIN
==================================================

Please build / revise Ken's progression around this chain:

Ken
→ Lounge becomes possible
→ Lounge is completed
→ Ken returns and experiences the finished Lounge
→ Ken proposes holding a wine tasting event
→ Ken personally hosts the tasting
→ tasting becomes an occasional Lounge event
→ Ken hosts THREE tasting events over time
→ after the third hosted tasting, Jill and Ken develop a Jill's Kitchen × Ken collaboration wine
→ the collaboration wine permanently enters the restaurant's wine list
→ Ken's later story, including Monsieur Du, continues naturally from this established history

This should feel like one continuous relationship between:
KEN + JILL + LOUNGE + WINE

not a collection of unrelated unlocks.

==================================================
KEN CANON
==================================================

Ken is 36.

He is a sommelier / wine professional.

He is intelligent, relaxed, mature, slightly playful.

His wine knowledge is already established.

DO NOT write a story about discovering why Ken knows wine.

DO NOT turn him into a tutorial NPC.

DO NOT make him lecture Jill or the player about basic wine knowledge.

The point is:

After the Lounge exists, we finally get to SEE Ken actually doing what he is good at.

His professional competence should reveal more of his personality.

==================================================
BEAT 1 — KEN RETURNS TO THE FINISHED LOUNGE
==================================================

Soon after the Lounge is completed, Ken should have an authored follow-up.

He comes into the actual finished Lounge.

Let him experience the space naturally:
- sit at the bar / Lounge
- order wine
- look around
- notice how Jill actually built and uses the space

Do not make this a long congratulatory scene.

Avoid:
「終於完成了。」
「這裡真的很棒。」
「妳實現夢想了。」
generic emotional exposition.

The important outcome is that Ken sees the real space and starts thinking about what could happen here.

This leads naturally into his suggestion.

==================================================
BEAT 2 — KEN PROPOSES A WINE TASTING
==================================================

Ken himself should suggest:

Why not hold a small wine tasting here?

Not a giant event.

Something appropriate for Jill's Kitchen Lounge:
roughly 6–8 guests.

Example tone only, NOT mandatory exact dialogue:

Ken：「這裡其實可以辦品酒。」
Jill：「在這裡？」
Ken：「嗯。不用很多人。六個、八個就夠了。」
Jill：「你要辦？」
Ken：「我可以主持。」

Keep the dialogue understated and natural.

Ken is proposing something because he can genuinely imagine doing it here.

==================================================
FIRST WINE TASTING
==================================================

The first tasting MUST be an authored story event.

The player should actually SEE Ken hosting.

This is important.

Do not reduce it to:
"Wine tasting completed +$X"

For the first time, the player should see a different side of Ken:
professional, comfortable, confident, but still recognizably Ken.

He should not suddenly become a stiff lecturer.

For example, his hosting can be lightly playful:

Ken：「不用猜產區。先喝。」

Someone immediately starts guessing.

Ken：「我就知道一定有人不聽。」

This is only tone guidance.
Write dialogue that fits existing Ken canon.

Jill / Lounge staff can naturally be present because they are working.

==================================================
IMPORTANT PRESENTATION RULE
==================================================

This is an AUTHORED / NON-MISSABLE story event.

If it occurs during service:

PAUSE ACTIVE RESTAURANT GAMEPLAY.

- gameplay time freezes
- order/service pressure freezes
- dialogue does NOT auto-advance
- player taps/clicks to advance
- resume the exact service state after the scene

Do not make the player manage customers while trying to read Ken's story.

Ambient tasting behavior may happen in realtime, but important authored dialogue must be player-controlled.

==================================================
AFTER FIRST TASTING — RECURRING WORLD EVENT
==================================================

After the authored first tasting succeeds, unlock:

KEN'S WINE TASTING NIGHT

as an occasional Lounge event.

Do NOT create a giant event-management system.

Keep it lightweight and integrated into existing restaurant systems.

Example Today News:

今晚｜Ken 的品酒夜 · 8 席

During a tasting:
- Ken must actually be present
- he is the host
- appropriate Lounge seats may be reserved
- guests drink wine
- paired dishes can naturally be ordered / served
- Lounge visually feels slightly different from an ordinary night
- the event may produce higher spend if consistent with the existing economy

The important thing is PERCEPTIBILITY.

The player should be able to tell:
"Tonight is Ken's tasting."

Not merely receive a hidden modifier.

==================================================
THREE HOSTED TASTINGS
==================================================

Ken must personally host THREE tasting events before the collaboration wine story becomes available.

Do not make all three identical.

They should show progression.

TASTING #1
- first trial
- authored introduction
- Ken establishes himself as host

TASTING #2
- the event is becoming familiar
- some returning guests / different wines / different food pairings / small variation
- Ken and Jill already understand the rhythm better

TASTING #3
- this now feels like something Jill's Kitchen actually does
- Ken and Jill have developed working shorthand
- it should feel established rather than experimental

Do not turn this into:
1/3
2/3
3/3

No quest counter UI.

The game may track the count internally.

The player should experience three events, not grind a visible progress bar.

==================================================
AFTER THIRD TASTING — COLLABORATION WINE
==================================================

After Ken has hosted the third tasting, trigger an authored follow-up.

This is the payoff.

Ken and Jill decide to make:

JILL'S KITCHEN × KEN
COLLABORATION WINE

The idea should emerge naturally from what they have been doing together.

Do NOT present it as an achievement popup.

A possible tone:

After closing / after the third tasting,
Ken is still at the bar.
A few glasses remain.

Jill：「三次了。」
Ken：「妳有在數？」
Jill：「你每次都說下次換一支。」
Ken：「那不要換了。」
Jill：「嗯？」
Ken：「做一支自己的。」

Again:
tone reference only.
Rewrite naturally if needed.

==================================================
WHAT "COLLABORATION WINE" MEANS
==================================================

Ken is a sommelier / wine professional.

Do NOT suddenly make Ken and Jill operate a winery.

Do NOT add vineyard/farming/winemaking simulation.

The collaboration can plausibly involve:
- Ken's professional wine connections
- working with an existing winery / producer / supplier
- selecting or developing an appropriate wine/style
- Jill contributing the restaurant / food-pairing perspective
- choosing what fits Jill's Kitchen
- finalizing the bottle as their collaboration

Keep the production side believable but lightweight.

The story is about:
Ken + Jill having done this together enough times that they create something lasting.

==================================================
PERMANENT WORLD CONSEQUENCE
==================================================

When the collaboration is complete:

The collaboration wine PERMANENTLY enters the Jill's Kitchen wine list.

This is important.

The story should leave something behind in the restaurant.

It can have:
- its own wine-list entry
- short description
- food pairing(s)
- reasonable gameplay effect/value consistent with the existing wine system

But:

DO NOT make it absurdly overpowered.

Its emotional value is that the player knows where it came from.

Normal customers may order it later.

Ken may occasionally order / comment on it.

Future tasting nights may include it.

Monsieur Du may eventually have his own opinion about it if appropriate.

The collaboration wine should become part of the restaurant's ordinary life.

==================================================
MONSIEUR DU CONNECTION
==================================================

Please inspect the existing Ken × Monsieur Du 8-beat story.

Do NOT destroy or rewrite good existing material unnecessarily.

But make sure it now follows naturally from Ken's established Lounge life.

Monsieur Du should not feel like:

"Randomly, Ken now has another story."

If compatible with existing canon, one elegant entry point is a later Ken tasting.

Ken is hosting.

Monsieur Du is attending / present.

He hears Ken say something about a wine.

Monsieur Du makes a restrained comment or disagreement.

Ken notices him.

Do NOT write this as dramatic rivalry introduction.

No:
「看來遇到對手了。」
「有意思。」
anime rival clichés.

They are two mature men who know wine.

The interest should come from specificity, restraint and professional recognition.

If the existing Ken × Du story already has a stronger canonical first meeting, preserve it and connect the tasting history another way.

==================================================
USE EXISTING SYSTEMS
==================================================

IMPORTANT:

We just added:
- wine-list research
- wine / food pairing
- tasting-related upgrades/training
- wine cellar
- Lounge
- Lounge staff

Use these systems where appropriate.

Do NOT build duplicate systems.

The point is to make existing restaurant systems acquire narrative meaning.

For example:

Wine research should no longer feel completely disconnected from Ken.

Wine pairing can matter during tasting nights.

The wine cellar can become relevant later if already owned.

But:

DO NOT hard-lock the core Ken story behind expensive optional upgrades unless narratively necessary.

The player built the Lounge.
That is the main prerequisite for this next chapter of Ken.

==================================================
DESIGN PRINCIPLE
==================================================

This is the model I want:

STORY
→ changes the restaurant
→ the changed restaurant creates new situations
→ those situations create more story
→ story leaves permanent traces in everyday gameplay

Ken helped create the Lounge.

Therefore the Lounge should create the next chapter of Ken.

After three tastings, Ken and Jill create the collaboration wine.

The collaboration wine then remains in the restaurant even when no story scene is playing.

That is the feeling I want.

Characters should NOT disappear after functioning as unlock keys.

Use existing major-story pacing rules.

Do not let unrelated story scheduling permanently starve this line.

If a valid Ken major beat is displaced by another major beat, preserve the existing next-day priority / anti-starvation behavior.
