==================================================
1. CORE STORY PRINCIPLE
==================================================

Do not design the Second Floor as:

Day X
→ expansion unlocked
→ pay money
→ second floor

Do not design it as:

cats discover upstairs
→ second floor unlocked

Do not design it as:

Staff Room problem
→ second floor unlocked

Instead:

CUSTOMERS notice the restaurant could physically grow upward.

STAFF become aware of the unused upstairs space and occasionally talk about it.

JILL has mundane contact with the landlord / upstairs space.

THE CATS accidentally create the first real exploration of the space.

STAFF and CUSTOMERS continue generating different spatial pressures.

The player remembers that the unused floor exists.

Only after enough time and actual lived evidence does Jill seriously consider leasing it.

The Second Floor should feel like an idea that slowly becomes obvious.


==================================================
2. WHAT THE SECOND FLOOR IS
==================================================

There is an existing second floor above Jill’s Kitchen.

It belongs to the landlord / building property.

It is currently unused / unleased and is NOT part of Jill’s Kitchen.

It is NOT:

- Jill’s secret property
- an abandoned former Jill’s Kitchen
- an old family restaurant
- a mysterious locked floor
- a sentimental archive
- a ruined building
- a haunted space
- a hidden inheritance
- a secret expansion room

It is simply:

「房東那邊還空著的二樓。」

Earlier in Jill’s Kitchen’s life, Jill had no reason to pay for another whole floor.

The restaurant was smaller.

Now the restaurant is changing.


==================================================
3. THIS MUST BE A LONG ARC, NOT ONE EVENT
==================================================

Spread the Second Floor arc across many game days.

Conceptually:

PHASE A
Customers first make the player aware of upstairs.

PHASE B
Staff casually acknowledge that upstairs exists.

PHASE C
Jill has a mundane reason to inspect it with / through the landlord.

PHASE D
Days later, during normal restaurant life, the upstairs door is accidentally not fully latched.

PHASE E
At closing, 柔柔 and 小齁 are discovered missing.

PHASE F
Staff and Jill immediately search for them.

PHASE G
The search leads everyone upstairs.

PHASE H
The cats are found safely exploring the empty second floor.

PHASE I
The restaurant returns to normal. No expansion unlock yet.

PHASE J
Over later days, customer and staff space problems continue.

PHASE K
Different people independently reference upstairs again.

PHASE L
Jill eventually realizes the second floor now has an actual use.

PHASE M
Jill approaches landlord.

PHASE N
Player gets the major paid Second Floor expansion project.

PHASE O
The WHOLE second floor becomes part of Jill’s Kitchen.

PHASE P
Only later do Staff Room and Private Dining Room emerge as separate story-driven uses of areas upstairs.

==================================================
9. CRITICAL CONTINUITY RULE FOR THE CAT EVENT
==================================================

Do NOT write:

Jill inspected upstairs several days ago,
forgot to close the door,
and only several days later noticed cats missing.

That is not believable for people living / working with five indoor cats.

Instead:

Jill inspected upstairs several days earlier.

The upstairs access was properly closed afterward.

Then on THE DAY OF THE CAT EVENT,
the door / latch becomes imperfectly closed again through a mundane reason.

Possibilities depending on architecture:

- Jill briefly accesses it again
- landlord / maintenance access occurs
- the latch is imperfect and does not fully catch
- someone closes it but it does not fully latch

The door appears closed enough that staff do not immediately notice.

Later it opens slightly / remains just wide enough.

柔柔 gets through.

小齁 follows.

They have been missing only for a believable short period:

minutes to perhaps around an hour or two.

NOT days.
