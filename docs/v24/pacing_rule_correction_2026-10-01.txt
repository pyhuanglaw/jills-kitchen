ONE MORE PACING RULE CORRECTION

Keep this rule:
- At most ONE major authored story beat may fire on the same in-game day.

But REMOVE any global rule such as:
- different stories must be separated by at least 2 days;
- every major beat needs a multi-day cooldown;
- a story cannot continue on the following day.

That kind of global cooldown is too mechanical and can make natural story continuity feel artificially delayed.

Pacing should be determined by narrative causality, not a fixed cooldown.

For example:

Day X:
阿珠姐's phone conversation reveals that 怡君's new home has a leak.

Day X+1:
If Sophie and Mia naturally visit and the required characters are actually present, it is perfectly valid for them to ask about it / see the photos / begin helping.

Do NOT force an empty day merely because a generic “2-day story gap” rule says so.

Likewise, some events SHOULD require longer gaps when the fiction requires time:
- waiting for another rainfall;
- professional inspection;
- collecting documents;
- arranging formal legal representation;
- scheduling mediation;
- completing repairs;
- allowing a move-in to feel settled.

So the intended rule is:

NARRATIVE TIME > GLOBAL COOLDOWN.

Use:
- same-day maximum: 1 major authored beat;
- story prerequisites;
- actual character presence;
- event-specific minimum delays only where the fiction requires them.

Do NOT use:
- universal 2-day separation between different stories;
- universal multi-day cooldown between beats.

The purpose of pacing is to make events feel like they happened over time, NOT to make the player wait for the story system.

Also, unrelated ambient life may continue normally between or alongside authored beats.
