The existing Day ~27 operational upgrade currently called 「後場休息室」 conflicts with the future story premise that staff do not yet have a real place of their own.

- a place where staff can comfortably sit for a long time;
- a room with a closable door;

- the future Staff Room.

- Hugo may still end up sitting on a box;
- staff meals may still lack a proper place;
- bags / coats / chargers may lack a real home;

Preserve the old upgrade's purchased state and mechanical benefit in legacy saves.

Do not refund / remove / rebuy it.
