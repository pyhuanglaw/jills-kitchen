JILL'S KITCHEN — NEW LONG-FORM CROSS-CHARACTER STORY ARC
阿珠姐 × Sophie × Mia × 王先生
Working title: 《那面牆》

IMPORTANT:
This is a genuine long-form story arc, NOT a collection of ambient callbacks or relationship flavor events.

It should unfold across many in-game days, with:
- setup
- discovery
- uncertainty
- investigation
- professional involvement
- setbacks / incomplete evidence
- formal legal handling
- mediation
- compensation
- aftermath

The player should genuinely want to know:
「怡君買的房子到底怎麼了？」
and later:
「這件事情最後能不能處理掉？」

Do NOT compress this into 3–4 events.

==================================================
0. STRICT CONTINUITY / PREREQUISITES
==================================================

This story happens ONLY AFTER 怡君 has already:

1. appeared naturally as 阿珠姐's adult daughter;
2. completed her earlier housing story 《三個選項》;
3. chosen and purchased a home;
4. moved into that home;
5. lived there for some time.

Do NOT reveal the leak during the home-selection arc.

The earlier arc should feel COMPLETE first.

The emotional effect should be:

玩家原本已經看著怡君選好房子、搬進去，
以為這件人生小事已經告一段落。

過了一段時間，生活又發生新的事情。

Do not retroactively turn 《三個選項》 into obvious foreshadowing for a defective house.

The home choice was reasonable based on what 怡君 knew at the time.

This new story should begin only after enough time has passed that 怡君 genuinely feels she has moved in.

==================================================
1. CHARACTER / OCCUPATION CANON FOR THIS ARC
==================================================

阿珠姐
- Senior Jill's Kitchen kitchen employee.
- 怡君's mother.
- Practical, experienced, not sentimental or theatrical.
- She worries about her daughter but initially does NOT want to turn her daughter's problem into restaurant gossip.

Sophie
- Establish her occupation as media / magazine / editorial work.
- She understands how to reconstruct a timeline, preserve source material, ask precise questions, and eventually recognize when a private incident may point to a broader public-interest / consumer-life topic.
- She is NOT a tabloid exposé machine.
- She should understand that publishing accusations before facts are established can create problems.

Mia
- Establish her occupation as interior / spatial design or a closely related built-environment profession.
- She can recognize suspicious repair / repainting / moisture-related physical signs.
- She is NOT a building inspector and must never magically diagnose a leak from one photograph.
- Her professional judgment creates useful hypotheses that still require evidence / inspection.

王先生
- Existing regular customer.
- Lawyer.
- He is a real practicing professional, not a free legal-advice NPC.
- Once formally retained, he charges a real legal fee.
- He ultimately assists 怡君 through formal handling / mediation and helps obtain compensation.

Do NOT change these professions merely to fit another system.

==================================================
2. EXISTING SOPHIE / MIA / 阿珠姐 RELATIONSHIP
==================================================

Before this arc begins, establish / preserve that 阿珠姐 has already noticed Sophie and Mia are together.

阿珠姐 has a subtle 「姨母笑」 reaction to them.

She does NOT loudly announce:
「我早就知道妳們在交往！」

Instead, over ordinary visits, she has clearly figured it out.

Example tone:

阿珠姐：「今天怎麼只有妳？」
Sophie：「她加班。」
阿珠姐：「喔～～」
Sophie：「妳那個喔是什麼意思？」
阿珠姐：「沒有啊。」

They occasionally chat.

This matters because Sophie and Mia later care about 阿珠姐's problem for a believable reason.

They are not strangers suddenly performing a side quest for an employee.

==================================================
3. OPENING — SOMETHING IS WRONG
==================================================

After 怡君 has been moved in for some time:

Sophie and Mia notice 阿珠姐 seems distracted / worried during several visits.

They ask.

阿珠姐 initially refuses to explain.

Example:

Sophie：「阿珠姐，妳最近怎麼了？」
阿珠姐：「沒怎樣。」
Sophie：「妳明明就有。」
阿珠姐：「吃妳的飯啦。」

Do not reveal everything immediately.

On a later day, Sophie and Mia happen to hear part of a phone call between 阿珠姐 and 怡君.

Only fragments at first:

阿珠姐：
「妳先拍起來。」
「不是擦掉就好了啦。」
「上次不是才叫人來？」
「……好啦，妳先不要弄。」

Afterward, they learn the basic situation:

怡君 has discovered a significant water-leak problem AFTER moving into the home she purchased.

The leak may appear / worsen during rain.

The other side has not simply accepted responsibility.

There is uncertainty about:
- whether the defect existed before sale;
- whether it had previously been repaired;
- what the seller knew;
- what was disclosed;
- where the water is actually coming from.

Do NOT immediately establish:
「seller knowingly committed fraud。」

That must remain uncertain.

==================================================
4. MIA BECOMES INVOLVED FIRST THROUGH HER PROFESSION
==================================================

Mia asks to see photographs.

She notices signs that make her suspicious that this area may have been repaired before:
- localized repainting;
- patching;
- finish differences;
- moisture / repair patterns;
or another technically plausible visual clue.

But she must explicitly remain cautious.

Example:

Mia：「這裡以前可能處理過。」
Sophie：「所以他們本來就知道？」
Mia：「不是。這只能說可能處理過。」
Sophie：「差在哪？」
Mia：「差很多。」

IMPORTANT:
Mia does NOT prove legal knowledge.
Mia does NOT replace a professional inspection.
Mia does NOT magically solve the source of the leak.

Her role is to notice:
「這件事情可能沒有表面上那麼簡單。」

==================================================
5. SOPHIE'S PROFESSIONAL ROLE
==================================================

Sophie begins helping reconstruct what actually happened.

She asks whether 怡君 still has:
- original listing material;
- viewing photographs;
- messages;
- disclosure documents;
- repair conversations;
- dates;
- handover records;
etc.

She helps turn a confused family complaint into a chronological record.

This should feel like an editor / media professional instinctively organizing facts.

Sophie may initially think:

「如果真的早就知道還不講，這件事其實可以寫。」

But she does NOT immediately publish anything.

Her eventual article comes much later.

==================================================
6. 王先生 OVERHEARS
==================================================

王先生 should NOT be summoned as a quest NPC.

He is already a regular customer.

On one ordinary visit, he happens to overhear enough of their conversation to recognize that this may be a real property-sale dispute.

Example:

Sophie：
「所以看屋的時候那面牆才剛漆過？」

Mia：
「如果照片日期沒錯的話。」

阿珠姐：
「可是對方就說不知道啊。」

王先生 pauses.

王先生：
「等一下。」

They look at him.

王先生：
「妳女兒買的是中古屋？」

This is his natural entrance into the arc.

He begins asking lawyer-type questions:
- purchase / handover timing
- contract
- disclosure / current-condition documents
- when the leak first appeared
- what was said in writing
- photographs
- repair history
- what exactly the seller has admitted or denied

He should immediately distinguish suspicion from proof.

For example:

Sophie：「如果交屋前就補過，還能說不知道嗎？」

王先生：
「先不要跳那麼快。」

Mia：
「可是那面牆一定處理過。」

王先生：
「處理過，跟知道現在這個漏水問題，是兩件事。」

Mia：
「我知道。」

王先生：
「那就先證明第一件。」

==================================================
7. IMPORTANT — 王先生 DOES NOT WORK FOR FREE
==================================================

At first, 王先生 can casually point out what information should be preserved and warn them not to make unnecessary statements.

But once the matter requires actual legal work, make the boundary explicit.

He should effectively say:

「如果要我處理，就正式委任我。」

怡君 is the client / actual party.

There should be a real lawyer-client relationship and a real fee.

王先生:
- reviews the documents;
- helps determine the legal position;
- communicates / negotiates where appropriate;
- prepares for mediation;
- participates in the mediation process.

DO NOT make him a magical pro bono lawyer simply because he is a regular at Jill's Kitchen.

==================================================
8. THE FEE IS DISCOUNTED — BUT 王先生 NEVER ANNOUNCES IT
==================================================

This is important characterization.

王先生 gives 怡君 a genuinely favorable / discounted fee.

But he NEVER says:

「我算妳友情價。」
「我有打折喔。」
「因為阿珠姐所以算便宜。」

Do not make him seek credit for generosity.

The characters infer it themselves.

For example, 怡君 has already consulted / asked about fees elsewhere.

When she sees 王先生's formal fee:

怡君：
「……這比我之前問的低很多欸。」

阿珠姐：
「真的？」

怡君：
「嗯。」

阿珠姐 looks at 王先生.

阿珠姐：
「你是不是算太少？」

王先生：
「沒有。」

阿珠姐：
「真的？」

王先生：
「妳女兒有付錢就好。」

Stop there.

Do not explain the emotion.

The player should understand the discount without 王先生 saying it.

IMPORTANT:
The fee must still feel like a REAL professional fee.
Not a symbolic NT$1 / NT$3,000 joke.
怡君 genuinely pays for legal representation.

==================================================
9. INVESTIGATION / SETBACKS
==================================================

Do not make every clue support 怡君.

The story needs uncertainty.

Possible structure:

A viewing photograph initially seems extremely favorable.

Sophie thinks:
「這不是就有了？」

Mia looks closer and realizes it does NOT establish what they hoped.

Or:

A repair mark exists, but the repair may not initially be proven to concern the same water source.

Or:

The first assumed source of the leak turns out to be wrong after inspection.

The player should experience at least one moment where:

「喔，原來沒有這麼簡單。」

王先生 should repeatedly separate:
- what they suspect;
- what they know;
- what they can prove.

This is part of his professional characterization.

==================================================
10. MEDIATION IS THE CLIMAX
==================================================

The legal storyline should NOT end with:
「王先生幫忙處理，後來成功了。」

Actually depict the mediation as an authored story beat.

王先生 is visibly different in professional mode.

He does not need to shout or deliver a courtroom speech.

His strength is:
- organized facts;
- knowing which evidence matters;
- identifying weak points;
- controlling the discussion;
- negotiating realistically.

A good 王先生 line:

「那我們一項一項談。」

The evidence assembled through the arc matters here.

Mia's observations helped identify what needed examination.
Sophie helped reconstruct and preserve the chronology / source material.
怡君 provided the actual documents and evidence.
王先生 converts the usable material into a legal strategy.

The mediation ultimately reaches a settlement.

怡君 receives REAL COMPENSATION.

The compensation should be meaningful relative to the repair problem.

Do not necessarily make it 100% of everything she originally wanted.
A negotiated result can feel more credible.

The important result is:

怡君 can actually repair / address the home without personally absorbing the entire unexpected loss.

==================================================
11. SOPHIE'S ARTICLE COMES AFTER THE LEGAL MATTER IS SAFE
==================================================

Do NOT use:
Sophie writes exposé → seller panics → pays.

That cheapens both Sophie and 王先生.

After mediation / settlement has progressed or concluded, Sophie returns to the broader issue.

She realizes 怡君's experience points toward a useful consumer / housing story.

She asks before using anything connected to 怡君.

Example:

Sophie：
「我想寫這個。」

怡君：
「寫我的？」

Sophie：
「不一定寫妳。是寫這件事。」

Her eventual piece should be broader:
- buying a used home;
- hidden water leaks;
- disclosure;
- documenting conditions;
- preserving evidence;
- what buyers often fail to photograph / retain;
etc.

It should feel like a real piece of her professional work that grew from something she encountered through Jill's Kitchen.

Do not make it revenge journalism.

==================================================
12. AFTERMATH — DO NOT OVERWRITE THE EMOTION
==================================================

After compensation / repair:

Do not have characters say:
「大家互相幫忙真的很溫暖。」
「Jill's Kitchen 就像一個大家庭。」
「幸好有你們。」
「這就是熟客的意義。」

Never explain the theme.

Instead use behavior.

王先生 later comes in for an ordinary meal.

阿珠姐 gives him a noticeably generous portion.

王先生：
「今天是不是比較多？」

阿珠姐：
「有嗎？」

王先生：
「有吧。」

阿珠姐：
「快吃啦。」

End.

No speech.

==================================================

==================================================

Much later, if / when Jill's Kitchen begins serious Second Floor renovation planning:

Mia looks at the space.

Mia：
「先看漏水。」

Sophie laughs.

阿珠姐：
「真的，先看。」

If 王先生 happens to be present:

王先生：
「……這次跟我沒關係。」

This callback should ONLY occur after 《那面牆》 has actually completed.

Do not make it required for Second Floor progression.

The Second Floor story remains independent.

This is shared history, not a dependency.

==================================================

==================================================

Target this as a long arc across many in-game days.

Conceptually:

Do not fire these events on consecutive days.

Allow ordinary restaurant life between them.

The player should occasionally wonder:
「怡君那個漏水後來怎麼樣了？」

==================================================
15. JOURNAL / SYSTEM RULES
==================================================

This is a meaningful authored character / relationship story arc.

Use the existing story / Journal architecture.

Do NOT:
- create a quest checklist;
- show evidence 3/7;
- show legal progress meters;
- create a leak minigame;
- make the player collect documents manually;
- make the player choose legal strategy;
- add lawyer affection;
- add relationship points.

The player witnesses / follows the story.

The characters handle their own adult lives.

The player's reward is:
- knowing these people better;
- seeing their lives continue;
- seeing relationships between restaurant staff and regular customers become real history;
- later recognizing callbacks.

==================================================
16. CHARACTER WRITING RULE
==================================================

This arc is specifically meant to solve a current Jill's Kitchen weakness:

Employees and regular customers must NOT feel like two separate NPC databases.

This story should create genuine shared history between:

阿珠姐
Sophie
Mia
王先生

But do NOT force them into a permanent friend group afterward.

After the story:
- Sophie and Mia remain customers;
- 阿珠姐 remains an employee;
- 王先生 remains a regular;
- they simply know each other differently because something real happened between them.

That distinction matters.

Do not make everyone best friends.

==================================================
17. DO NOT OVERWRITE 怡君
==================================================

怡君 is the actual homeowner and legal client.

She must retain agency.

阿珠姐 must NOT make legal decisions for her.
Sophie must NOT publish her story without permission.
Mia must NOT decide what repairs she should accept.
王先生 must NOT decide settlement terms without her.

The others help because they care / have relevant expertise.

怡君 decides:
- whether to retain 王先生;
- whether to mediate;
- whether to accept the settlement;
- what Sophie may use / disclose.

She is not a helpless daughter being rescued by her mother's friends.
