IMPORTANT PRODUCT / STORY ARCHITECTURE UPDATE

Please pause the current Staff Room progression design before locking its spatial/unlock architecture.

We have made an important upstream story decision:

SECOND FLOOR must come BEFORE Staff Room.

Do NOT implement:

Staff Room → later discover/unlock second floor

and do NOT implement:

Staff Room → Private Room as a building tech tree.

Instead, Second Floor becomes the larger Restaurant Story arc and physical expansion that creates the space in which Staff Room and later Private Dining Room can naturally exist.

If current work has already implemented reusable Staff Lives infrastructure, character facts, authored beats, callbacks, ambient events, tenure logic, outside visitors, etc., KEEP IT.

Do not restart or discard good work.

Only revise the spatial/story dependency that determines where and when Staff Room appears.


==================================================
1. SECOND FLOOR PREMISE
==================================================

Jill’s Kitchen currently occupies the restaurant floor.

There is an existing second floor above the restaurant that belongs to the landlord / building property but is not currently part of Jill’s Kitchen’s usable leased restaurant space.

It has simply been left unused / unleased.

This is NOT:

- Jill secretly owning an abandoned floor
- a mysterious locked room
- a hidden inheritance
- an old Jill’s Kitchen floor
- a dramatic secret
- a ruined haunted space
- a floor that must be unlocked piece by piece

It is simply:

「房東那邊還留著的二樓。」

A completely believable piece of the building that Jill’s Kitchen has never needed before.

At the beginning, Jill has no strong reason to rent or take over another whole floor.

The restaurant was smaller.
The existing space was enough.

Over time, that changes.


==================================================
2. SECOND FLOOR MUST BE FORESHADOWED BEFORE IT BECOMES A PROJECT
==================================================

Do not suddenly add:

SECOND FLOOR EXPANSION AVAILABLE

after a Day threshold.

Players should become aware that the second floor exists before they are allowed to expand into it.

Use several low-frequency authored/ambient moments across many days.

Do not fire them consecutively.


==================================================
3. FIRST FORESHADOW — SOMEONE MENTIONS UPSTAIRS
==================================================

Use an existing long-time customer / regular whose canon makes sense after auditing the source.

A customer may casually notice or ask about upstairs.

For example:

客人：
「樓上也是這間的？」

Jill：
「不是，房東留著的。」

客人：
「一直空著？」

Jill：
「好像是。」

END.

No Story Update.

No expansion notification.

No dramatic camera.

No:
「Maybe someday...」

This should initially feel like ordinary information about the building.


==================================================
4. CUSTOMERS BEGIN TO NOTICE THAT THE RESTAURANT COULD GROW UPWARD
==================================================

Much later, after Jill’s Kitchen has visibly become busier and more established, another customer may independently notice the same physical possibility.

For example:

熟客：
「妳們現在人這麼多，沒想過樓上？」

Jill：
「樓上不是我們的。」

熟客：
「租啊。」

Jill：
「你講得很簡單。」

END.

This is not the customer giving the player a quest.

It is simply the first time someone says aloud what the player may already have started thinking:

The restaurant could physically grow upward.

Do not immediately unlock anything.


==================================================
5. STAFF MAY ALSO REFER TO UPSTAIRS NATURALLY
==================================================

Staff can occasionally mention it once the restaurant is mature enough.

Examples:

Nina:
「樓上真的一直都空著喔？」

Jill:
「嗯。」

or:

Hugo:
「上面多大？」

Jill:
「不知道。」

Nina:
「妳沒看過？」

Jill:
「沒有啊。」

These should be sparse.

Do not make every character suddenly obsessed with the second floor.


==================================================
6. THE CATS ARE THE FIRST ONES TO ACTUALLY CLAIM IT
==================================================

At some later point, there should be an authored event where Jill gets legitimate access to look at the second floor.

Possible reason after source/architecture audit:

- landlord is showing Jill the space
- landlord allows Jill to inspect it after she asks
- landlord mentions it is still unused
- another mundane property-management reason

Keep landlord involvement ordinary and believable.

No villain landlord.
No rent dispute.
No melodrama.

Jill opens the second-floor space for the first time.

The space should feel:

- empty
- structurally usable
- dusty / unused enough to show it has been vacant
- not destroyed
- not full of mysterious restaurant relics
- clearly not yet Jill’s Kitchen

There may be basic leftover neutral building items, but do NOT invent sentimental Jill’s Kitchen history upstairs.

Most importantly:

ONE OR MORE OF THE FIVE CATS GET INSIDE.

The cats must remain indoors.

They are not outside and they do not leave the building.

They simply take advantage of an open upstairs door / stair access and explore before Jill intended them to.

This should feel funny and inevitable.

Example:

Jill:
「欸。」

Cat keeps walking upstairs.

Jill:
「……回來。」

It does not.

Another cat may follow.

Do not make all five cats march upstairs in formation.

Use actual cat presence / autonomous behavior if architecture allows.

Do not teleport absent cats solely for the cutscene.


==================================================
7. CAT EXPLORATION IS AN IMPORTANT EMOTIONAL FORESHADOW
==================================================

The cats entering upstairs should NOT instantly turn the second floor into a cat room.

The point is:

For the first time, the player sees this empty floor with something from Jill’s Kitchen already moving through it.

The restaurant has not expanded yet.

But visually, the player can imagine it.

A cat may:

- inspect a corner
- sit by a window
- sniff an empty doorway
- look downstairs
- briefly settle somewhere Jill did not expect

Jill eventually gets them back downstairs.

No permanent cat furniture yet.

No unlock.

No Story Photo automatically.

This event should create anticipation.


==================================================
8. SECOND FLOOR STORY SHOULD DEVELOP ACROSS TIME
==================================================

After Jill has actually seen upstairs, normal restaurant life continues.

Do not immediately build it.

Over subsequent days, existing problems make the empty second floor increasingly relevant:

- staff have nowhere appropriate to rest
- staff meals struggle for space
- relatives/friends wait for employees
- private staff belongings lack a place
- some customer groups want more private dining space
- mature restaurant capacity / circulation becomes increasingly constrained
- regulars occasionally mention growth
- Jill’s Kitchen is visibly no longer the small restaurant it once was

These existing systems should feed one shared Restaurant Story.

The player should gradually reach:

「樓上真的可以用了吧。」

before Jill formally acts.


==================================================
9. FORMAL RESTAURANT STORY — SECOND FLOOR
==================================================

Only after enough actual observed evidence should the formal Restaurant Story advance.

Do not base this only on Day number.

Eligibility should combine things such as:

- restaurant maturity
- economic maturity
- customer volume / established restaurant state
- meaningful staff tenure
- at least some observed staff-space problems
- at least one customer comment / spatial-growth observation
- Jill has actually inspected upstairs
- cat exploration event has occurred
- sufficient spacing since those events

Exact thresholds should fit existing architecture after audit.

Then Jill can finally contact / speak with the landlord about taking the second floor.

Keep dialogue understated.

Example direction:

房東：
「妳真的要用樓上？」

Jill：
「嗯。」

「現在下面不夠？」

Jill looks toward restaurant.

「開始有一點。」

That is enough.

Do not write a motivational speech.


==================================================
10. THE PLAYER GETS THE WHOLE SECOND FLOOR
==================================================

This is the key correction.

When the player commits to the Second Floor expansion:

THE SECOND FLOOR ITSELF becomes Jill’s Kitchen space.

Do NOT divide the narrative into:

unlock first half
→ one light
→ unlock second half
→ second light

Do NOT use the previous “two lights” concept.

Discard that idea.

The player takes over / leases / expands into the WHOLE usable second floor as one major restaurant expansion.

It should feel significant.

This is a mature-game capital investment.

Audit the mature-save economy and set a meaningful cost.

It should be one of the moments where the player thinks:

「餐廳真的變大了。」


==================================================
11. IMPORTANT: WHOLE FLOOR DOES NOT MEAN EVERY ROOM IS FINISHED
==================================================

Once the second floor becomes part of Jill’s Kitchen, the whole floor is physically accessible / owned by the restaurant’s simulation.

But individual rooms / uses can still develop through later stories.

This distinction is essential:

SECOND FLOOR
= physical expansion / new restaurant floor

STAFF ROOM
= one later use of part of that floor

PRIVATE DINING ROOM
= another later use of part of that floor

Possible future flexible space
= reserve architecture only if useful; do not invent unnecessary content now.

Do not represent unfinished areas as magically inaccessible halves of the floor.

They can simply be:

- empty
- lightly furnished
- awaiting purpose
- temporarily unused

The player should understand:

「二樓已經是我們的，只是還沒有全部想好要拿來做什麼。」
