Please treat this as the canonical design brief for the next Staff Life / Staff Room expansion.
Before implementation, audit the existing source for all staff identities, ages if defined, portraits, roles, tenure, dialogue, staff meals, new-hire/veteran events, Story Facts, Journal architecture, event arbitration, room architecture, save schema and existing Lounge expansion logic.
Existing source canon wins over any accidental contradiction in this brief. If an age/name/detail conflicts, preserve existing canon and adapt the new material. Do not silently replace existing character facts.
CORE PRODUCT IDEA
The system is Staff Lives, not merely Staff Room.
Employees continue to have lives outside Jill’s Kitchen. The player gradually discovers those lives because people, habits, schedules and small consequences occasionally cross the restaurant boundary.
Staff Room is a major Restaurant Story consequence of this system, not its starting point and not a level-based shop unlock.
Do NOT create an employee-management simulator.
No morale, fatigue, affection, needs, employee quests, claim rewards or daily maintenance chores.
STORY ARCHITECTURE
Separate:
1. Canonical facts — true in the world but not necessarily known by player/NPCs.
2. Introduced facts — actually revealed through a real event.
3. Authored beats — one-time meaningful events with eligibility and presence checks.
4. Callbacks — only legal after their prerequisite event really happened.
5. Ambient consequences — low-frequency evidence that established lives continue.
Canon knowledge must never automatically become NPC knowledge.
A character cannot refer to a relative/friend/event that has not actually been introduced in that save.
Reuse the existing Story Facts/history/event-arbiter architecture rather than building a second relationship engine.
Presence must be real. Never teleport absent characters into a scene.
CORE STAFF CAST
Build the first Staff Lives layer around:
阿珠姐, Marco, Nina, Hugo, 小彤, 阿德師傅, 秀琴阿姨, Kai.
晴×阿拓 already have a substantial relationship arc and should remain available as staff-room occupants, but do not let Staff Room become another 晴×阿拓 romance system.
阿珠姐
Approx. 54 unless existing canon says otherwise.
Veteran cook. Adult daughter 怡君, 27, working and living independently.
Arc:
1. 《搬家》 — 阿珠姐 asks for a slight schedule adjustment because her daughter is moving.
2. 《吃飯啊》 — days later 怡君 visits with a friend.
   阿珠姐：「妳怎麼來了？」
   怡君：「吃飯啊。」
   阿珠姐：「附近那麼多間。」
   怡君：「不能吃妳工作的喔？」
3. 怡君 later returns alone as ambient consequence.
4. After Staff Room exists, 怡君 brings a practical object 阿珠姐 previously mentioned the room needed. The object becomes a persistent visual trace.
5. Much later 怡君 may bring her boyfriend. Do not turn this into approval drama.
   Nina：「那是怡君男朋友？」
   阿珠姐：「嗯。」
   Nina：「妳看過了？」
   阿珠姐：「看過。」
   Nina：「怎麼樣？」
   阿珠姐：「吃飯。」
Marco
Approx. 35. Experienced restaurant worker with previous workplaces.
Old colleague Kevin, approx. 36, still works in restaurants.
Arc:
1. 《以前一起做事的人》 — Kevin visits and intentionally orders an inconvenient dish Marco is handling. Marco sees ticket:「……白痴。」
2. Later visit establishes their old-industry familiarity.
3. High-tenure milestone 《待滿久了》:
   Kevin：「你這裡待滿久了。」
   Marco：「嗯。」
   Kevin：「不走了？」
   Marco：「目前沒有。」
Never add a speech about finally finding a home.
Nina
Approx. 26. Fast, observant, remembers people's habits. Not “the gossip girl.”
Long-time high-school friend 珊珊, same age, quieter/slower temperament.
Arc:
1. 《等妳》 — 珊珊 first waits for Nina after work.
   Jill：「妳朋友？」
   Nina：「高中同學。」
   珊珊：「她以前很安靜。」
   Jill：「誰？」
   Nina：「妳可以走了。」
2. Later scene shows Nina is naturally quieter around her oldest friend.
3. After Staff Room, 珊珊 can sometimes wait inside.
4. Later coworkers naturally recognize her only after real introductions.
Hugo
Approx. 31. Highly reliable while working; comically low-power while resting. He is not stupid.
Younger brother 宇翔, 22.
Arc:
1. 《我弟》 — 宇翔 brings something to Hugo. Coworkers discover Hugo has a younger brother.
2. Small later scene reveals Hugo naturally handles a practical problem for him; do not verbally explain that Hugo is caring.
3. After Staff Room:
   Hugo：「你進去等我。」
   宇翔：「可以喔？」
   Hugo：「可以。」
4. Later ambient references to 宇翔's internship/graduation/life progression.
小彤
Approx. 22, living with parents, early stable working life.
This is a long time-progression arc.
1. Existing/new-hire uncertainty: asks where things are; unsure whether she may sit during breaks.
2. 《你們怎麼來了》 — parents visit restaurant.
   小彤：「你們怎麼來了！」
   爸爸：「吃飯啊。」
3. 《想很久了》 — after earning stable wages, she buys an ordinary personal item she had wanted for months. Do not frame as wealth achievement.
4. Staff Room: initially Jill has to tell her「坐啊。」 Over time she gains a habitual place/cup.
5. Very late 《第二格》 — a new employee asks where something is. 小彤 answers immediately:「下面第二格。」
This is the completion of her transformation from new hire to someone who knows the restaurant.
阿德師傅
Approx. 47. Quiet, stable routines.
Long-time fishing friend 老林, approx. 50, more talkative.
1. 《等一下》 — 老林 comes to wait for 阿德; there is nowhere appropriate to wait. This can contribute Staff Room evidence.
2. Later player sees 阿德 talks much more naturally with 老林 than at work.
3. After repeated real visits, staff may naturally begin calling him「林叔」.
4. 《今天阿德沒上班》 — eventually 老林 visits even when 阿德 is absent and still eats there. He has become part of Jill’s Kitchen’s own social world.
秀琴阿姨
Approx. 58, legacy staff who has watched restaurant grow.
Husband 國雄, approx. 60. Ordinary long marriage; no melodrama.
1. 《來接妳》 — 國雄 first appears waiting to pick her up.
2. 《下雨》 — on a rainy day Jill invites him to sit while waiting.
3. After Staff Room, Jill can tell him to wait inside.
4. 《今天不想煮》 — much later 國雄 comes alone.
   秀琴：「你來幹嘛？」
   國雄：「吃飯。」
   秀琴：「家裡不能吃？」
   國雄：「今天不想煮。」
Kai
Approx. 29. Regular badminton group outside work. No major trauma/romance/family arc is needed.
1. 《星期三》 — Kai asks not to be scheduled Wednesday evening because he plays badminton.
2. 《四個人》 — four badminton friends visit.
   Kai：「你們小聲一點。」
   Friend：「服務態度很差欸。」
3. Later callback about his badminton result.
The four friends do not need four independent story arcs.
STAFF ROOM RESTAURANT STORY
Staff Room cannot simply appear in the upgrade shop.
Accumulate real staff-space evidence across different event types:
- Hugo sits on a box after rush because there is nowhere to sit.
  Jill：「你幹嘛坐那裡？」
  Hugo：「這裡可以坐。」
  Jill：「……那是箱子。」
- a relative/friend has nowhere appropriate to wait
- staff meal ends with people searching for somewhere to sit
- personal coats/bags/water bottles/chargers lack a proper place
Do not unlock based only on Day number or staffCount.
Eligibility should combine restaurant maturity + active staff + tenure + at least one outside-life visitor actually introduced + at least two different categories of staff-space evidence.
Then trigger the Restaurant Story beat:
《大家待的地方》
Jill observes after closing:
「店裡有客人的位置。
有貓的位置。
有吃完飯還不想走的人待的位置。
好像一直沒有一個地方，
是給每天在這裡工作的人待的。」
This unlocks the paid Staff Room Project.
STAFF ROOM PROGRESSION
Design three meaningful paid stages suitable for a mature Day-50+ economy.
Staff Room I — 有地方坐了
Basic seating, table, storage, water/basic break space. Unlocks room-life scenes.
Staff Room II — 開始像他們的地方
Lockers, small refrigerator, better seating, outlets, tea/coffee area and persistent personal objects.
Staff Room III — 真的有人生活過
Better sofa/table, small audio setup, shelf/storage and richer personal traces.
Do not make it luxurious. It should look accumulated and lived-in, not like a corporate lounge.
Upgrades should create visible spatial changes, not only numbers.
ROOM ECOLOGY
Build low-cost autonomous behaviors rather than scripted scenes every visit:
- 阿德 develops a preferred seat.
- Hugo sometimes opens refrigerator, forgets what he wanted, closes it, then returns.
- Nina remembers where people's things are.
- Marco often listens without joining.
- 小彤 gradually develops her own seat/cup.
- 阿珠姐 first complains there are not enough outlets; later the room visibly proves her right.
- 秀琴 sometimes brings excess food from home.
- Kai's badminton bag may occasionally appear when he is working that day.
These should not generate constant notifications.
SOCIAL-WORLD CROSSOVER
Outside characters must eventually stop behaving like isolated quest NPCs.
Once legitimately introduced, they may gradually recognize other staff.
Examples:
- Nina eventually recognizes 怡君.
- 小彤 may casually speak with 宇翔 because they are close in age.
- 國雄 and 老林 may once overlap while waiting.
- 老林 eventually becomes a restaurant visitor independent of 阿德.
Keep these interactions sparse and natural. Do not turn everyone into best friends.
JOURNAL
Meaningful authored beats may appear under existing 人物／關係支線.
Ambient hints/callbacks should NOT inflate denominator.
Staff Room itself belongs to 餐廳故事 because it changes the restaurant.
Preserve current chapter architecture unless audit shows a clean extension point. Do not create a giant new employee quest UI.
LIFE ALBUM
Ordinary visits can be captured organically by normal Life Album if the real characters are present.
Do not generate Story Photos for every relative/friend introduction.
Reserve one possible future authored photo concept, 《休息一下》, for a much later lived-in Staff Room scene. Do not implement/generate it yet; first see whether actual play makes the moment worthy.
WRITING RULE
Emotion should come from behavior, shorthand, routine and changed space.
Avoid explanatory sentimental dialogue such as:
- 「這裡真的像家。」
- 「大家已經不只是同事了。」
- 「我終於找到屬於自己的地方。」
- 「謝謝 Jill 給我們一個家。」
Prefer lines like:
「插座不夠。」
later:「我就說吧。」
or:
「你進去等我。」
「可以喔？」
「可以。」
Never explain an emotion the player can already infer.
IMPLEMENTATION / QA
First audit the existing game against this brief.
If existing canon conflicts, adapt this design rather than overwrite canon and document the change.
If there is no substantive product decision requiring me, proceed directly from audit to implementation without stopping for approval.
Do not consume every arc in one release. Implement enough early/mid beats and infrastructure that mature saves immediately begin revealing the system while preserving later events for long-term play.
Migration must be safe for mature saves. Existing saves must not receive a flood of retroactive story events on load.
