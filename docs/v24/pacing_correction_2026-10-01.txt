PACING CORRECTION — COMPRESS MUCH MORE

The current proposed pacing is still far too slow for actual play.

This is a restaurant-management game with many parallel systems, not a real-time daily-life simulator. A mature Day 52 player should NOT need another 50–80 in-game days before the Second Floor storyline even begins.

Revise the chronology to approximately:

1. 怡君 introduction → 《三個選項》 → purchase / move-in / spare-key payoff:
   TARGET ~7–10 in-game days total.

2. After move-in:
   allow ~2–4 ordinary restaurant days so the move feels completed and settled.

3. 《那面牆》:
   TARGET ~12–18 in-game days total.

Do NOT force the previously conceptual 18 phases into 18 separate authored events.

Consolidate them into approximately 8–10 meaningful player-facing beats.

For example:

Beat 1 — 阿珠姐 seems worried / phone fragments / leak becomes known
Beat 2 — Mia examines available visual evidence; Sophie begins reconstructing the timeline
Beat 3 — early evidence / seller response creates the first working theory
Beat 4 — 王先生 naturally overhears and identifies the legal issue
Beat 5 — an apparently useful clue becomes weaker / more ambiguous than expected
Beat 6 — stronger evidence / professional findings emerge; 怡君 formally retains 王先生
Beat 7 — preparation / negotiation develops
Beat 8 — mediation
Beat 9 — settlement / compensation
Beat 10 — Sophie article + quiet aftermath, which may itself be split or partially ambient if appropriate

Some beats may naturally occur on nearby days.

Do NOT insert artificial 2–4 day waits between every beat merely to make the arc “long-form.”

Long-form means:
the story develops, changes direction, and has remembered history.

It does NOT mean:
the game makes the player wait unnecessarily.

Keep:
- at most one major authored story beat per in-game day;
- no event dump;
- ordinary gameplay between major beats where it feels natural.

But remove any global rule requiring every important beat to be separated by multiple days.

4. After 《那面牆》 resolves:
   only ~2–4 ordinary days are needed before early Second Floor awareness can begin.

IMPORTANT:
“Second Floor storyline begins” does NOT mean immediate acquisition.

The Second Floor storyline already contains its own pacing:

Therefore DO NOT use 怡君 / 《那面牆》 as an artificial pacing wall for Second Floor content.

《那面牆》 begins.
